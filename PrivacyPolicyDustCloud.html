Privacy Policy — Dust Cloud

Last updated: 3 September 2026
Developer: PPER
Contact: alperpper@gmail.com

Dust Cloud is a paid, offline-first game. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no user accounts. You do not sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

What is sent off your device

The game uses two analytics and configuration services. Neither receives information that identifies you personally.

GameAnalytics (analytics)

Used to understand how the game is played so we can fix difficulty spikes and bugs. It receives:

  • a randomly generated identifier for the installation
  • device model, operating system version, and app version
  • approximate country, derived from your IP address
  • gameplay events: session start and end, progression through the game, currency earned and spent, and named events such as claiming an achievement or performing the first “melt”

GameAnalytics is provided by GameAnalytics ApS. Their privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy

Firebase (analytics and remote configuration)

Provided by Google. Used for two purposes:

  • Remote Config lets us correct game balance — prices, prestige rewards — without shipping an app update. The app downloads a short list of numbers.
  • Analytics records basic app usage such as sessions and app version.

Firebase receives a randomly generated app instance identifier, device and operating system information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads and include no advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not ask for permission to do so.
  • We do not sell or share your data with data brokers.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data. The app never requests these permissions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Stop all collection: uninstall the app. No data remains on your device and none continues to be sent.
  • Request deletion: email alperpper@gmail.com with your device model and approximate first-use date. We will ask GameAnalytics and Google to delete the records associated with your installation identifier. Because we do not store personal identifiers, this is the only way we can locate them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address before the change takes effect.
